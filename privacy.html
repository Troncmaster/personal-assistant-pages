Privacy policy

Personal Assistant is a private application used by one person, its owner, on the owner's own computer. It is not available to anyone else and collects no data about other people.

What it accesses

With the owner's consent through Google sign-in, it has read-only access to the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar (the gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly scopes). It cannot send, draft, change or delete anything.

How the data is used

Email and calendar content is read on the owner's computer, and only to tell the owner about their own mail and events, for example a summary of new messages or an upcoming appointment. Message bodies are fetched when needed and are not kept. Short notes derived from them, and the owner's preferences about which senders matter, are kept in an encrypted database on the same computer. Notifications about the owner's own mail may be delivered to the owner's own private messaging account.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train generalised AI models. Any language model that reads it runs locally on the owner's computer.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Personal Assistant's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions and delete the locally stored data from their own computer.
